## A flexible canvas for a project story

This sample page is a visual inventory of the case-study blocks available in the portfolio. The
interface artwork and sample writing are illustrative—not a client project or a claim about shipped
work.

### Headings, paragraphs, and lists

Use `RichText` for narrative sections. Write in Markdown, starting with a level-two heading beneath
the project title, then use paragraphs, subheadings, and lists to shape the story.

- **Context:** explain the setting, audience, and constraints.
- **Approach:** describe the design decisions and evidence behind them.
- **Outcome:** share only verified, publishable results.

</RichText>

## Pairing a visual with supporting copy

Use a split layout to keep a related explanation close to its image. The image can sit on either
side; the next example switches its position.

</RichText>

### Give the detail a purpose

Explain what the viewer should notice and why it matters. This column supports headings and
paragraphs alongside the image without forcing every case study into the same rhythm.

</SplitImage>

<SplitImage
  src={wideInterface}

### Reverse the composition

The same block can place the image on the left. Alternate sides only when it helps pacing or makes
the relationship between copy and image clearer.

</SplitImage>

## Choosing a sequence

Combine only the blocks that help explain the work. Reorder them to match the real process: a story
can move from context to comparison, from a full-width visual into a split detail, or from a quote
to a final full-bleed image.

</RichText>
